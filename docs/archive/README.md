# Archive — research and evidence, not specification

This directory holds the discovery material the original front-desk design was drawn from:
site-visit documents, the earlier contract drafts, redacted patient-call transcripts and working
notes. None of it is normative. The contents are kept on disk but are **not committed** (see
`.gitignore`) because they contain patient conversations and real hospital names, numbers and staff
data; nothing here may be used as fixture data or on a live call. The current integration authority
is the pinned owner contract in `docs/handover/mcp-only/contracts/`.
